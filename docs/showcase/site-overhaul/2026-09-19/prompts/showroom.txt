Use case: ui-mockup.
Create one realistic, production-quality UI design for an overhaul of the Dethink Components public showcase website. Direction name: SHOWROOM — discover complete interfaces by the job you are trying to do.
Target dimensions: 1440 x 1024 desktop website viewport, landscape. ONE focused primary website screen, not a board of screens, not a device mockup. Fill the canvas with the site.
Attached references: the actual current recipe gallery and homepage. Preserve the Dethink/Components brand mark and developer-library identity. Build on the actual Relay research landing and AI Chat Studio visual styles visible in the gallery. This is a broad redesign; the hierarchy should be strikingly different from a component directory. Current date anchor 2026-09-19; inventory 67 components and 19 recipes. Do not invent customer counts, testimonials, prices, sign-ins or unavailable products.
Art direction: a beautifully restrained editorial product gallery. Warm off-white page, deep charcoal typography, dark forest-teal actions, pale sand dividers. Large confident serif headline paired with crisp Inter-like 14–16px UI text (maximum two fonts). Generous margins, confident asymmetry, precise alignment. Website previews provide the imagery and color. No grid-paper backdrop, decorative 3D blobs, gradients, rounded app container around the whole page, or a wall of pill tags.
Header: Dethink/Components left; Recipes selected, Components, Guides; Search icon and compact theme control at right.
Compact editorial introduction across the top third: eyebrow "REAL INTERFACES. OPEN CODE." Strong headline "What are you building?" and one line "Start with a complete flow. Make it your own." Under it, a simple horizontal workflow selector "All examples", "AI apps", "Dashboards", "Marketing", "Settings". Use a subtle underline for selection, no huge filter-card.
The hero use case is one large featured recipe across the middle: left two-thirds is a crisp, carefully framed screenshot of the REAL Relay landing direction from the reference, paper-white with cobalt serif heading "From scattered sources. To a clear next move.", small working-style CTAs and top of the research workspace. Do not reinterpret Relay as an unrelated software. On the right, editorial copy outside a card: small "FEATURED · MARKETING", heading "Relay research landing", sentence "A research product, from first impression to source-backed answers." One solid primary button "Explore recipe" and a quiet "See components →" link. Under that, a short unboxed line "Built with Tabs, Card, Dialog + 5 more". Maintain lots of breathing room.
Below this featured row, a fine rule and a supporting strip "More starting points" with two equally weighted small landscape previews beside each other: "AI Chat Studio" showing a clean conversation workspace and "Settings & billing" showing a tidy settings and plan view. Concise titles and one short line each; no long text descriptions, complexity badges, or nested cards. Let these be secondary to the featured recipe.
A short low-emphasis bridge near the bottom: "Need a single building block? Browse all 67 components →". This keeps the primitive path easy to find.
Use spacing, grouping, type, and dividers before borders or elevation. Treat this as an inspirational yet highly usable public developer website, not a portfolio or SaaS sales page. Exactly one primary action in the frame. Text must be legible and precise. The word SHOWROOM may appear as a tiny lower-right concept stamp; no numeric option label.
