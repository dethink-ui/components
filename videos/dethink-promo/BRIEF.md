## Intent

Striking, fast-paced 30-second motion-design promo for Dethink Components — a shadcn-compatible,
open-code React component library for production SaaS dashboards, internal tools, B2B apps and
AI-native interfaces. Sell, not tour. A rapid-fire montage of real components from the live gallery
(buttons, data tables, charts, carousel, aurora background, inspector, timeline…) cut on the beat,
with stat hits (90+ components, shadcn-compatible) — built to make the viewer want to open the gallery.
