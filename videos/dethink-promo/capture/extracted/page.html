General

Buttons, links, labels, and text.

  • ButtonRun an action, such as saving a form or opening a dialog.
  • Icon ButtonIconButtonShow an action as an icon when space is limited.
  • Button GroupButtonGroupKeep related buttons together in a row or column.
  • Dropdown ButtonDropdownButtonOpen a menu of related actions from a button.
  • Reveal ButtonRevealButtonShow an icon that reveals its label on hover or keyboard focus.
  • BadgeShow a short status, label, or count.
  • LinkTake users to another page or a section on the same page.
  • TypographyStyle headings and body text with consistent sizes, weights, and colors.

Layout

Spacing, alignment, grids, and dividers.

  • BoxWrap content with spacing, a background, a border, or rounded corners.
  • ContainerCenter page content and limit its width.
  • StackArrange content in a row or column with even spacing.
  • FlexArrange items with control over alignment, wrapping, and available space.
  • GridArrange content in rows and columns.
  • SeparatorAdd a horizontal or vertical line between sections.
  • Resizable PanelsCompose, resize, focus and restore useful workspaces.

Disclosure

Expandable sections and switchable views.

  • AccordionLet users expand and collapse sections of content.
  • Horizontal AccordionHorizontalAccordionExpand one panel at a time in a horizontal row.
  • TabsSwitch between related views without leaving the page.

Forms

Labels, text fields, and choice controls.

  • Form FieldFormFieldConnect a form control to its label, help text, and error message.
  • InputCollect a single line of text, such as a name or email address.
  • TextareaCollect several lines of text, such as a message or description.
  • Number InputNumberInputCollect a number with optional minimum, maximum, and step values.
  • SliderChoose a value, range, or labelled milestone with an accessible slider.
  • CheckboxLet users turn individual options on or off.
  • Radio GroupRadioGroupLet users choose one option from a visible list.
  • SelectLet users choose one option from a dropdown list.
  • ComboboxLet users search a list and choose an option.
  • SwitchTurn a setting on or off.

Advanced Inputs

Searchable lists, tags, dates, and audio input.

  • File UploadFileUploadStage files in a compact queue with clear limits and recoverable uploads.
  • Async SelectAsyncSelectLet users choose from options loaded by your app, with loading and retry states.
  • Multi SelectMultiSelectLet users search for and choose several options.
  • Tag InputTagInputLet users add, edit, and remove text tags.
  • Voice InputVoiceInputCapture microphone audio and show its input level.
  • Date PickerDatePickerLet users type a date or choose one from a calendar.
  • Date Range PickerDateRangePickerLet users choose a start date and an end date.
  • Date & Time PickerDateTimePickerLet users choose a date and time.

Feedback

Loading indicators, status messages, and notifications.

  • Feedback StatesShow loading, progress, errors, empty states, and notifications.

Overlays

Dialogs, menus, tooltips, and panels.

  • DialogOpen a focused window above the page for a task or confirmation.
  • PopoverShow extra content next to a button or other trigger.
  • TooltipShow a short hint when users hover over or focus a control.
  • Dropdown MenuDropdownMenuShow a menu of actions when users open a trigger.
  • DrawerOpen a panel from the edge of the screen.

Navigation

Menus and controls for moving around an app.

  • BreadcrumbShow where the current page sits in the site hierarchy.
  • Command PaletteCommandPaletteLet users search for actions and pages from one place.
  • Navigation MenuNavigationMenuGroup page links in a menu with optional dropdown panels.
  • NavDockShow navigation links in a compact icon dock.
  • PaginationLet users move between pages of results.
  • SidebarOrganize app navigation in a sidebar that can collapse.
  • Sidebar ActivitySidebarActivityKeep background work, progress, and attention items visible in a sidebar.
  • Bottom BarBottomBarCompose a collapsible bottom work panel with custom content and sizing.
  • Sidebar ShellSidebarShellArrange a sidebar, header, main content, and footer into an app layout.
  • StepsShow the current step and progress through a task.

  • CalendarLet users choose a date or date range from a month view.
  • CardGroup related content and actions in a single panel.
  • CarouselLet users browse a series of cards or images.
  • Card ScrollerCardScrollerLet users scroll through and select cards in a row.
  • Card StackCardStackLet users browse cards arranged in a stack or fan.
  • Card Stack AnimatedCardStackAnimatedDirectional card decks with optional touch navigation.
  • AvatarRepresent a person or team with an image or initials.
  • Avatar GroupAvatarGroupShow a group of people with overlapping avatars.
  • Data TableDataTableDisplay rows of data with sorting, filtering, selection, and pagination.
  • Slot PlannerSlotPlannerManage available time slots and let users request bookings in their time zone.
  • TableDisplay data in rows and columns.
  • TimelineShow events in order with statuses and optional selection.
